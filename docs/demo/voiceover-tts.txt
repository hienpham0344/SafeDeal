Every P2P crypto deal has a trust paradox: send Avalanche upfront and risk getting scammed; deliver your work first and risk getting ghosted; or use a centralized escrow and go back to trusting a middleman. Meet SafeDeal — non-custodial P2P escrow on Avalanche, where smart contracts hold the funds, R-F-C 87-85 locks the terms, and no admin can ever touch your funds.

Flow 1: Cooperative Trade. The seller drafts deal terms. SafeDeal hashes them deterministically with R-F-C 87-85 in both TypeScript and Rust, then anchors the terms hash on Avalanche Fuji. The buyer funds the vault, the seller marks delivery, and the buyer releases payment. Notice SafeDeal uses Pull-Payment withdraw — funds are credited to claimable balances rather than pushed inline, eliminating reentrancy and reverting-contract attacks.

Flow 2: Open-Buyer and Refund Protection. Need a public payment link? Leave the buyer address blank — the first wallet to fund locks in as the buyer. And if a seller misses the delivery deadline, or issues a voluntary refund, the buyer reclaims 100 percent of their funds directly on-chain without asking anyone for permission.

Flow 3: Dispute and 2-of-3 Consensus. When a deal goes wrong, participants upload private evidence validated by binary magic-bytes and gated by Sign-In with Ethereum — outsiders get a strict 403 error. Unlike traditional escrows where a single arbiter has god-mode, SafeDeal requires 2-of-3 matching votes across the buyer, the seller, and the arbiter. And if everyone ghosts? After 14 days, anyone can trigger a timeout resolution for a deterministic 50-50 split — zero trapped funds.

Built with Solidity plus Foundry invariant fuzzing, a Clean Architecture Rust backend on Railway, and a reload-safe React UI live on Avalanche Fuji. Try SafeDeal today at avalanche-9b099.web.app.
